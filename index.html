The ACE Club

About Us

ACE Club is a non-profit tutoring orginization ran entirely by local high school students. We have a wide range of tutors who can offer both academic assistance and private music lessons to your elemetary schooler. Our mission as a non-profit is to provide accessible and free tutoring for the children in our area, as well as providing chances for teenagers to learn professionalism, adaptability and communitcation skills. We believe that the smaller age gap between tutors and students a more comfortable and relatable learning environment, allowing students to be taught while also gaining a valuable relationship.

Our Location

Our operations are currently based out of Los Alamitos Elementary School, and we will be holding our lessons in classroom P1. We are directly working with Simons Band and Orchestra, and the Los Alamitos School as a whole.

What We Offer

Academics

In our academics tutoring (nicknamed our 'Hearts Division') , we offer every subjects for K-8 education. Our core subjects that every tutor is guaranteed to be equipped for are math, science, history, and english, both reading and writing. If you have a child with need for academic help from a different subject such as computer science, please reach out to us through one of our cofounder's emails or our booking page. We will try our best to find a tutor suited to your needs.

Music

In our music division, (nicknamed our 'Spades Division'), unlike academics, the lessons that are offered are directly dependant on who our tutors are and their skill sets. The more tutors we gain, the more instruments we will be able to add to this list! However, listed here are the instruments we currently offer:

  • Violin
  • Viola
  • Cello
  • Flute
  • Piccolo
  • Drums
  • Piano
  • General Music Theory
