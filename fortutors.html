For Aspiring Tutors

This page is dedicated to recruiting and helping new tutors for our organization! if you are a parent, you may be looking for our tutor profiles instead of this page.

For Pioneer High School Students

If you attend Pioneer High School and want to become a tutor, please attend our weekly meetings in P5 every week during lunc. There, we will be able to sign you up and establish you as a tutor within our database. We will need to know your strengths and your ability to interact professionally with parents and children alike. To insure professionalism within all our tutors, we will try to invite teachers and others who are used to working with children to speak during club meetings. Most club meetings will be spent working out schedules and preparing resources and curriculum for tutoring sessions.

The Sophomore Project

Hours spent tutoring within the ACE Club will count for your Sophomore Project hours. However, we advise not to completely rely on ACE to finish all of your hours. We cannot yet guarantee the amount of children who will be attending our tutoring sessions, and How successful they will be at large.

For Non-Pioneer Students

If you do not attend Pioneer High School and want to become a tutor, simply reach out to us through our email attached at the bottom of the page. We are always in search of more tutors for our organization. However, there are still some prerequisites needed before you can join. In particular, we are looking for high school students, so if you are any younger than 14, we cannot allow you to join. Additionally, we will still need to know your strengths and your ability to professionally interact with children and parents.
