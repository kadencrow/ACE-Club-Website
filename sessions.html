How ACE Works

How we at ACE assign children to tutors is based on what area the child needs help with. Our tutors have specific strong suits and skill sets, so if your child needs help specifically in reading, or in math, you would recieve a tutor that is strong in that particular subject. Additionally, if your child tends to be more disruptive, or dislikes certain methods of teaching, we can find a tutor with greater communication skills, or with that certain style of teaching for you, so long as you let us know of these attributes. You can let any ACE representative know about these traits before any session and we'll write it down!
